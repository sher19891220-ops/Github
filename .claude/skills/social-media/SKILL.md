# Social Media Skills

Create and schedule social media content for transportation businesses and tech products.

## Triggers
- /social
- /social:post
- /social:linkedin
- /social:instagram
- /social:schedule
- "create a social media post"
- "write a LinkedIn post"
- "content calendar"
- "post about [topic]"

## Platform Rules

### LinkedIn (primary for TMS/freight)
**Audience**: Freight brokers, shippers, fleet managers, logistics VPs, investors
**Best performing content**:
- Behind-the-scenes: "Here's how we moved 47 loads last week across 12 states"
- Data/insights: freight rate trends, lane analysis, market commentary
- Founder journey: building TMS, lessons from transportation ops
- Driver stories (humanize the business)
- Industry takes: FMCSA changes, ELD mandates, fuel prices

**Format**:
- First line must hook (no "I'm excited to announce")
- Short paragraphs, line breaks for mobile
- 3-5 hashtags max, at the end
- 900-1300 characters optimal
- CTA at end (comment, DM, link)

### Instagram (fleet/driver community)
**Audience**: Drivers, owner-operators, trucking enthusiasts
**Content**: truck photos, loading/unloading, road trips, milestone posts, team culture
**Format**: Caption under 150 words, 15-20 hashtags in first comment

### Content Calendar Framework
Weekly rhythm for a trucking/TMS business:
- Mon: Industry insight or market data
- Wed: Behind the scenes / operations
- Fri: Win, milestone, or team highlight
