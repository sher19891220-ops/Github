## Skills

### /marketing:outreach — Shipper/Broker Outreach
Write cold outreach emails to freight shippers and brokers.

**Input**: Lane (origin → destination), commodity type, capacity available, rates
**Output**: 3 email variants (cold, follow-up, break-up email)

Rules:
- Subject line under 7 words, specific (lane or commodity, not "partnership opportunity")
- Lead with value in line 1: "We run Chicago→Dallas reefer 3x/week"
- One call to action only
- Under 150 words
- P.S. line for secondary hook

### /marketing:landing-page — Landing Page Copy
Write conversion-focused landing pages for TMS or trucking services.

**Structure**: Hero (headline + subheadline + CTA) → Pain points (3) → Solution → Social proof → Features → Pricing → FAQ → CTA

Rules:
- Headline: specific outcome, not category ("Book loads 3x faster" not "TMS Software")
- Subheadline: who it's for + how
- Features as benefits: "Automatic BOL generation" → "Never fill out a BOL by hand again"
- CTA: action verb + specific ("Start Free Trial" not "Get Started")

### /marketing:email — Email Campaign
Write email sequences for nurturing leads or retaining customers.

**Types**: Onboarding (5-email), win-back (3-email), product update, event announcement

### /marketing:campaign — Full Campaign Brief
Strategy document: target audience, channels, messaging, timeline, KPIs.

## Industry context

**Transportation/Freight:**
- Buyers: fleet owners, owner-operators, freight brokers, shippers (manufacturing, retail, food)
- Pain points: deadhead miles, cash flow (net-30+ payment terms), FMCSA compliance, driver retention
- Channels: direct outreach, DAT, load boards, trucking Facebook groups, LinkedIn (brokers/shippers)
- Seasonality: peak Q4, slow Q1

**TMS Software buyers**: dispatch managers, fleet owners 5-50 trucks, owner-operators scaling up
