🚚 Descartes Buys Its Way Into Latin America's Last-Mile Boom

Descartes Systems Group just acquired Santiago-based route-optimization platform Drivin for $30M, betting that AI-trained delivery data is the next competitive edge in last-mile logistics.

📊 The stats/facts:
• $30M upfront cash, plus up to $5M in performance-based earnout tied to revenue targets over the next two years
• Drivin is HQ'd in Santiago, Chile, serving distributors, retailers, CPG companies, and 3PLs across Latin America
• The platform uses AI/ML for route optimization, dispatch management, and real-time execution visibility in high-density urban markets
• Deal complements Descartes' existing fleet performance management suite and expands its Latin America footprint
• Announced July 6, 2026 — part of a wave of freight-tech consolidation moves this year

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Data-as-moat messaging: Descartes framed the deal around acquiring "real-world delivery execution data" to train AI models — a positioning template freight-tech marketers can borrow when selling their own platform's AI credibility
• LatAm is now a validated growth market for logistics tech — worth testing in targeting and ABM plans if you sell to carriers, 3PLs, or retail logistics teams
• The earnout structure signals confidence without overpromising — a useful model for how to frame your own company's acquisition or funding news
