🚛 Truck Capacity Just Hit a 43-Month High — and Rates Aren't Coming Down

ACT Research's June For-Hire Trucking Index shows the tightest capacity market since 2022, as regulatory driver rules and stalled equipment purchases collide with freight demand.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Capacity Index hit 55.0 in June, up 1.5 points — a 43-month high
• Freight Rate Index held at 70.2 (seasonally adjusted) after May's record 79.7 — still among the strongest readings in the survey's ~17-year history
• Driver Availability Index rebounded to 34.1 in June, but was at a five-year low of 30.4 in April after nondomiciled CDL restrictions took effect mid-March
• Only 47% of carriers plan equipment purchases in the next 3 months, well below the historical June average of 53%
• A six-month lag typically separates spot-rate gains from contract-rate gains — meaning shippers likely haven't felt the full pinch yet

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Urgency angle: capacity-constrained shippers are actively shopping for freight-tech, capacity-matching, and dedicated-contract solutions right now — "lock in capacity before it tightens further" is a live, defensible campaign hook, not hype
• Recruiting/compliance angle: with driver availability still fragile post-CDL crackdown, HR-tech, insurtech, and carrier marketers can lean into compliance-readiness and driver-retention messaging as a differentiator
• PR lesson: a single monthly index release (ACT Research) turned into sustained multi-outlet coverage across FreightWaves, Yahoo Finance, and trade press for over a week — proof that packaging hard data with a clear expert quote earns durable pickup, not just a one-day spike

🔗 Source: FreightWaves (Thomas Wasson), July 25, 2026, https://www.freightwaves.com/news/truck-capacity-shortage
