🚛 XPO's Freight Rebound Is Real — Q2 Adjusted EPS Jumps 62%

XPO just posted its strongest quarter in years, and the numbers suggest the long freight downturn may finally be turning the corner.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Q2 2026 revenue: $2.36B, up 13.2% YoY (from $2.08B in Q2 2025)
• Adjusted diluted EPS: $1.70, up 61.9% YoY (from $1.05)
• Operating income: $271M, up from $198M a year ago
• North American LTL adjusted operating ratio hit a record 79.9%, a 300-bps improvement YoY
• Adjusted EBITDA up 25% YoY (excluding real estate gains), per CEO Mario Harik

💡 Why marketers should care:
• The narrative is flipping from "survive the downturn" to "capture the recovery" — buyers who went quiet for two years are re-entering budget conversations, so re-engagement campaigns and dormant-lead outreach are timely again.
• A record operating ratio is a ready-made proof point — freight-tech, 3PL, and carrier marketers can point to XPO's efficiency gains as evidence that network/tech investment pays off even in a margin-tight market.
• Clean, numbers-forward earnings releases travel far beyond trade press into finance and general business media — a reminder that concrete metrics out-syndicate vague trend pieces every time.
