🚢 Jeddah Port Gridlock Triggers $500/TEU Surcharge as Red Sea Diversions Overwhelm Saudi Gateway

Carriers rerouting away from Strait of Hormuz risk have jammed Jeddah so badly that MSC just slapped a flat congestion fee on every box moving through it — and rivals are skipping the port entirely.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Yard utilization at Jeddah has hit ~90%, cutting terminal productivity 20-25%
• Truck queues outside the port stretch 5-6 km, with waits up to 3 days just to get in
• MSC introduced a $500 per TEU congestion surcharge covering all cargo types, equipment, and even existing service agreements
• Some carriers are dropping Jeddah from their schedules altogether, redirecting to King Abdullah Port, Djibouti, and Mundra
• Cause: Gulf land-bridge volume (Jeddah → truck → UAE/Oman/Qatar/Kuwait/Bahrain) kept climbing even after the Iran-US-Israel ceasefire eased Hormuz tensions

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Messaging hook: "resilience" and "route visibility" sell right now — freight-tech and insurtech marketers can lean into real-time diversion tracking and surcharge alerting as the pitch, not an abstraction
• 3PL/broker/carrier marketers on Middle East lanes have a proactive-comms opportunity: the brands that get ahead of customers with surcharge and ETA updates this week look like partners, not vendors
• PR lesson: MSC's fast, specific fee announcement is already shaping coverage — vague "we're monitoring the situation" statements cede the narrative to shippers venting about 3-day queues
