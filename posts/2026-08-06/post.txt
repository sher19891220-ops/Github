🚛 Forward Air Posts Best Quarter Since Its "Messy" Omni Merger — Stock Jumps 11%

Two and a half years after its rocky Omni Logistics acquisition tanked investor confidence, Forward Air just posted its strongest quarter yet — and Wall Street noticed immediately.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Revenue: $673M, up 9% YoY — the highest quarterly revenue in company history
• Adjusted EBITDA: $93M, up 18% YoY
• Expedited Freight segment operating margin: 10.9%, up 340 bps YoY, with tonnage up 7% YoY
• Omni Logistics segment adjusted EBITDA: $38M, up 28% YoY
• Despite a $244M non-cash goodwill impairment charge driving a GAAP operating loss, FWRD shares rose 10.7–11% in after-hours trading

💡 Why marketers should care:
• PR/framing lesson: a quarter containing a $244M impairment and a headline GAAP loss still got covered as a "turnaround" story — proof that leading investor/media narrative with operational metrics (margin gains, tonnage growth) over the scary GAAP number can flip sentiment overnight
• Targeting cue: tonnage +7% YoY and weight-per-shipment +5% YoY show shippers consolidating into fewer, heavier loads — a timing signal for freight-tech and capacity-matching vendors to target expedited/LTL carriers now
• Content angle: this is a ready-made "M&A integration done right (eventually)" case study for logistics-industry content aimed at executives evaluating their own merger playbooks
