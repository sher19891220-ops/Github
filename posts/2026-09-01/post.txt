🚨 DOT Just Shut Down 110 Truck Driving Schools Overnight

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy announced an emergency shutdown of 110 CDL training schools and flagged 160+ more for removal, part of a sweeping multi-agency fraud crackdown tied to English-proficiency violations and two fatal crashes.

📊 The stats/facts:
• 110 driving schools emergency-shut for 5,000 English-proficiency violations tied to their graduates
• 160+ additional schools issued removal notices from the federal Training Provider Registry
• 28,000+ commercial drivers placed out of service for English-proficiency violations since June 2025
• A Homeland Security Investigations surge is hitting 200+ schools across 23 states; DOJ launched "Joint Task Force Crossroads of America" (MI, OH, IN, IL)
• Two named schools (Platinum Plus in CA, ASC Technical Institute in TX) certified drivers later tied to fatal crashes

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Compliance is now a trust signal — carriers, driver-recruiting platforms, and training providers can win right now by leading with "audited," "compliant," and "verified credentials" messaging instead of generic hiring pitches
• This is a live case study in reputational risk: two schools went from local businesses to national news in one cycle because of safety failures — a reminder that PR/crisis-response readiness belongs in every carrier's and 3PL's marketing plan
• Insurtechs and safety-tech vendors (ELD, driver-vetting, training-verification tools) have a timely, urgent pain point to target fleet safety/compliance buyers with right now
