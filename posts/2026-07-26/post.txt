🎖️ White House Launches "Freedom Haulers" to Plug Trucking's Driver Shortage

The administration is recruiting veterans to replace the tens of thousands of immigrant truckers sidelined by its CDL/English-proficiency crackdown — and the numbers show just how big the gap is.

📊 The stats/facts:
• 26,000 drivers pulled off the road for failing English-proficiency tests
• 30,000 CDLs canceled since the crackdown began
• 40,000+ veterans already fast-tracked into trucking via a CDL skills-test waiver
• Truckers earn ~$70,000/year on average, per the Transportation Department
• GI Bill covers 100% of CDL training costs, plus a housing stipend, for vets with 3+ years of service

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Talent pipeline shift: veteran-focused recruiting (GI Bill benefits, base transition offices, "skip the skills test") is now a policy-endorsed growth channel for carrier/3PL/freight-tech hiring campaigns
• Messaging hook: concrete, benefit-driven angles ("skip the test," "100% covered training," "$70K average pay") are ready-made ad copy for HR-tech and staffing marketers targeting transitioning service members
• PR lesson: a punchy campaign name ("Freedom Haulers") drove the same story into 10+ national and regional outlets within 48 hours — proof a brandable name still wins earned media
