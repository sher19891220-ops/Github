🛢️ WHITE HOUSE ADVISERS ARE STUDYING A DIESEL EXPORT BAN AS PRICES HIT $6.51/GALLON

With diesel at a record high right at harvest season, Trump's top economic advisers are now formally analyzing whether a short-term ban on U.S. diesel exports would actually bring prices down — or backfire.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National average diesel hit $6.51/gallon on Sept. 23 — a fresh record, up from $5.61 just a month earlier
• NEC Director Kevin Hassett, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, and USTR Jamieson Greer are running the formal impact analysis at Trump's direction
• A leading proposal floated: a 90-day export ban, which the White House has publicly denied is a finalized plan
• Energy Secretary Chris Wright is pushing refiners toward a voluntary supply-boost alternative instead of a hard export ban
• Goldman Sachs warns a ban would fill U.S. storage and depress diesel prices only briefly — then shrink gasoline supply and push pump prices up

💡 Why marketers should care:
• This is a live, fast-moving policy story — carriers, brokers, and shippers are actively searching "diesel export ban" and "fuel surcharge" right now, making it a high-relevance newsjacking moment for freight-tech, fuel-hedging, and route-optimization brands
• The internal contradiction (White House denials vs. advisers actively studying it) is itself a messaging hook — content that clearly explains "what's actually being decided vs. rumored" earns trust with confused shippers and owner-operators
• PR lesson: this story's reach came from officials being named and quoted directly (Hassett, Bessent, Greer, Wright) — pairing your brand's take with specific, attributable sources beats vague industry commentary
