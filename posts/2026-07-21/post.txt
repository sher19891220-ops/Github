🚨 Houthis Declare Naval Blockade on Saudi Arabia — Key Red Sea Shipping Lane Now at Risk

Yemen's Iran-backed Houthi movement announced an immediate maritime blockade against Saudi Arabia at the Bab al-Mandeb Strait, reigniting the Red Sea shipping crisis and rattling ocean freight and insurance markets overnight.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Bab al-Mandeb Strait carries roughly 12% of global trade and about a quarter of all container shipping volume
• War risk insurance premiums jumped to ~0.75% of a ship's value, up from ~0.3% just before the announcement — a 150%+ spike in hours
• Comes on top of already-surging Asia-Europe ocean rates: ~$5,800/FEU to N. Europe and ~$7,200/FEU to the Mediterranean, up ~$3,000/FEU in six weeks
• Blockade declared in retaliation for a Saudi blockade of Yemen and a strike on Sanaa International Airport
• Threatens to disrupt one of the world's busiest shipping corridors and adds pressure to already-strained global energy markets

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Rate and capacity volatility is back as a headline pain point — freight-tech, insurtech, and visibility platforms have an immediate, timely hook for outbound messaging ("how exposed is your Q3 ocean freight?")
• Geopolitical risk is now a boardroom topic for shippers — content that translates it into concrete cost/ETA impact (not just news recaps) will outperform generic thought leadership this week
• Speed matters: the outlets winning attention right now are the ones publishing fast, sourced takes within hours — a lesson for brand/PR teams on newsjacking cadence during fast-moving supply chain events
