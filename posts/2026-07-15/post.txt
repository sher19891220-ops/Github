🚨 Freight Rates Just Hit a 14-Quarter High — And They're Still Climbing

Truckload and LTL pricing indexes both notched fresh multi-year highs in Q2 2026, and carriers are rolling general rate increases months ahead of schedule as capacity keeps tightening.

📊 The stats/facts:
• TL rate-per-mile index hit a 14-quarter high in Q2 — 16% above the Jan. 2018 baseline, projected to reach 17.7% (+11.7 pts YoY) in Q3
• LTL rate-per-pound index hit an all-time high in Q2 at 76.5% above the 2018 baseline, up 13.3 pts YoY
• ArcBest pushed a 5.9% GRI on June 22 — six weeks earlier than its 2025 timing
• Saia pushed a 7.1% GRI on July 6 — three months earlier than last year
• Diesel prices are running 51% higher YoY, with fuel surcharges over 60% above June 2025 levels; 48,000+ non-compliant drivers have exited the industry in the past year, tightening capacity further

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Rising rates are the #1 budget pain point in shippers' inboxes right now — freight-tech, insurtech, and 3PL marketers have a live, urgent hook: messaging around "protect your margin as GRIs land early" beats generic capacity pitches
• ArcBest and Saia moving GRIs weeks-to-months ahead of schedule is a lesson in cut-through comms: exact percentages and exact dates outperform vague "rates are rising" copy — mirror that specificity in your own outbound
• Carrier/broker/3PL marketers should treat this as a retention moment — reach out proactively to customers before they start shopping contracts elsewhere
