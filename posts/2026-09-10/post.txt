⛽ Diesel Just Shattered Its All-Time Record — And It's Still Climbing

The DOE/EIA weekly benchmark that most carriers use to set fuel surcharges just blew past the record set during the 2022 Ukraine-war oil shock, with no relief in sight.

📊 The stats/facts:
• DOE/EIA weekly average diesel price: $5.967/gallon, up 36.8¢ in a single week — this is the benchmark most fuel surcharge programs are pegged to
• AAA's daily average hit $5.9424/gallon, topping the prior all-time high of $5.82/gallon from June 2022
• SONAR's DTS.USA index reads $5.94/gallon; CME ULSD futures jumped 5.11% (+23.32¢) to $4.8010/gallon
• Analysts forecast diesel crossing $6/gallon within days
• Root cause: ~3M barrels/day of Arab Gulf refining capacity knocked offline by military action, plus 6-7M bpd of global production shut in with "no sign in sight" of resolution, per commodities analyst Jeffrey Currie

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Fuel surcharge transparency is now a live, urgent pain point for shippers — carriers, brokers, 3PLs and TMS/freight-tech vendors have a real, timely hook for "take control of your fuel exposure" messaging instead of generic thought leadership
• Insurtech and risk-management marketers can lead with the supply-route insurance gap Currie flagged — underwriting and product content around Gulf-transit risk will land right now
• When a macro commodity story is this top-of-mind for shippers, content that ties fuel volatility directly to rate/capacity forecasts will outperform anything generic this week
