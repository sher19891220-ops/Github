🚛 Class 8 Truck Orders EXPLODE 241% as Fleets Race the Clock

North American fleets placed 30,500 new Class 8 truck orders in June — the second-highest June on record — as buyers rush to lock in scarce 2026 production slots before they vanish and before tougher 2027 emissions rules hit.

📊 The stats/facts:
• 30,500 Class 8 truck orders placed in June 2026 — up 241% year-over-year
• Up 16% month-over-month from May, and ~68% above the 10-year June average
• Year-to-date orders now 125% ahead of 2025; the Sept 2025–June 2026 order season is running 36% above the prior season
• 12-month trailing order total: 334,160 units
• Analysts say remaining 2026 build slots could be fully committed by July, as fleets pre-buy ahead of the EPA's 2027 NOx emissions standards

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Urgency sells itself here: this is a textbook "act before the window closes" buying moment — OEM, fleet-finance/leasing, telematics, and insurtech marketers can build campaigns around limited 2026 slots and looming 2027 compliance costs.
• Narrow, high-intent window: fleets still shopping right now are unusually motivated buyers — a short runway for ABM outreach to fleet procurement and ops leaders before 2026 budgets and slots lock up.
• Borrow the regulatory-deadline hook: "compliance-driven surge" narratives travel well in trade press — pair urgency messaging with real filing/production deadlines (EPA 2027) instead of generic FOMO copy.
