🚛 Commerce Dept Proposes 25% Tariffs on Trailers & Freight Equipment

The Bureau of Industry and Security just opened a public comment period on new Section 232 duties covering 14 steel, aluminum, and copper derivative products — and semi-trailers and tanker trailers are on the list.

📊 The stats/facts:
• 25% proposed tariff on most of the 14 newly targeted derivative products, including semi-trailers and tanker trailers
• 14 total derivative product categories added, ranging from trailers and cranes to fire extinguishers and steel containers
• 15% carve-out rate for self-loading/self-unloading agricultural trailers
• 50% tariff on certain filled steel containers (LPG, oxygen, propene), applied to the container's value, not its contents
• Public comments due by August 27, 2026 — just a 3-week window before rules can move toward finalization

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Equipment costs are about to spike — carriers, fleets, and trailer OEMs facing a 25% cost jump are prime targets for "lock in pricing now" or "finance before the tariff lands" campaigns from lenders, lessors, and equipment sellers
• The comment window is a PR opening — trade-compliance and freight-tech vendors can publish timely explainers before Aug 27 and ride the same amplification Supply Chain Dive, KPMG, and trade press are already giving this story
• Cost-savings messaging beats feature pitches right now — shippers and 3PLs absorbing higher equipment costs will respond better to ROI-first positioning than feature-first pitches
