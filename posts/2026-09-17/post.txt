⛽ Diesel Hits $6.29/Gallon — Trucking's Breaking Point?

National diesel prices just hit a historic high, up 68% year-over-year, and analysts warn smaller carriers could start failing if the surge doesn't ease soon.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National diesel average: $6.29/gal on Sept. 14 — up 68.1% from $3.74 a year ago
• Brent crude hit $114.26/barrel (Sept. 9) amid Strait of Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb blockades
• U.S. refineries running at 97% capacity with low reserves — little room to boost output
• Drone strikes damaged Saudi Arabia's East-West Pipeline, raising fears oil could climb to $120-130/barrel
• Analysts warn of trucking company failures — stoppages, bankruptcies, and independents moving to large carriers

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Lead with ROI, not growth: freight-tech, fuel-hedging, and insurtech marketers should pitch cost-savings and risk-mitigation now — carriers are in survival mode, not expansion mode
• Segment the message: large contract carriers are insulated while small spot-market operators face existential pressure — tailor outreach and pricing to that split
• PR lesson: this story spread across a dozen+ outlets because trade press paired hard numbers with named experts making concrete forecasts ($120-130 oil) — attributable data drives syndication
