🚨 Cargo theft losses just DOUBLED — even as thefts went down

Verisk CargoNet's Q2 2026 report reveals a paradox: fewer cargo thefts, but organized crime is now hunting bigger scores — with losses topping $300M in a single quarter.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Q2 2026 losses hit $304.6 million — more than double Q2 2025's $135.7 million
• Theft incidents actually fell 26% year-over-year (down to 677 total)
• Average theft value: $564,009, driven by a handful of extreme multimillion-dollar hits
• Metal theft jumped from 54 to 80 incidents, with copper the top target
• Business email compromise remains the #1 access point — criminals impersonate trusted parties to redirect shipments

💡 Why marketers should care:
• The "fewer incidents, bigger losses" framing is a gift for insurtech and freight-security marketers — shift messaging from "theft volume" to "value at risk," which is the story shippers actually fear
• BEC-driven fraud is a digital trust problem, not just a physical-security one — TMS, visibility, and identity-verification vendors have a clear angle to own right now
• This is a masterclass in PR-as-content: Verisk turns a recurring internal dataset into a quarterly press hit picked up by a dozen trade outlets — a repeatable playbook for any freight-tech or carrier brand sitting on proprietary data
