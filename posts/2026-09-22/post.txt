🚨 16 Trucking Companies Just Filed for Bankruptcy — In Under a Month

Diesel just crossed $6/gallon for the first time on record, and small and mid-size carriers are buckling — a fresh wave of Chapter 7 and Chapter 11 filings just hit federal courts nationwide.

📊 The stats/facts:
• 16 trucking, delivery, and transportation companies filed for bankruptcy protection between late August and Sept. 21, 2026
• Split evenly: 8 Chapter 11 reorganizations, 8 Chapter 7 liquidations
• Filings span Texas, Illinois, Florida, Kansas, Arizona, Louisiana and beyond — hitting general freight, last-mile, ag hauling, and specialized carriers alike
• Comes as diesel hit $6.285/gal for the week ending Sept. 14 — the first reading above $6 in EIA records dating back to 2007, up 68% year-over-year
• Filers range from single-truck operators to fleets carrying $1M–$10M in liabilities

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Capacity is shrinking in real time — carriers, brokers, and freight-tech vendors should swap growth-mode messaging for resilience angles: cash flow protection, cost visibility, and "stay in business" positioning
• This is prime timing for insurtech/fintech/freight-tech marketers to target owner-operators and small fleets directly — factoring, fuel cards, and risk-management offers meet an audience that's actively searching for a lifeline right now
• PR/content lesson: FreightWaves turned routine public court filings into a data-driven trend story that got picked up industry-wide — proof that aggregating public records into a clear "wave" narrative is a reliable earned-media play
