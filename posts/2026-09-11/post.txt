🚨 US-Canada Trade War Escalates — Freight Volumes Already Cratering

The White House is banning a swath of Canadian imports outright, and cross-border freight demand is collapsing before the ban even takes effect.

📊 The stats/facts:
- Canada-to-US freight volumes down ~16% since Sept. 7, per FreightWaves SONAR data
- New US import ban (alcohol, motorcycles over 800cc, dairy, molasses) takes effect Sept. 29
- Canada's retaliatory tariffs (15%-50%) hit $20B of US goods — roughly 6% of annual US exports to Canada
- US tariffs expand to 50% on dozens more product categories starting Sept. 15
- $50B in Canadian products stripped from the GSA's federal contracting schedules

💡 Why marketers should care:
- The freight imbalance is a messaging opportunity: carriers and 3PLs moving capacity south with empty miles north need positioning that turns volatility into a service differentiator ("we absorb the swings so you don't have to").
- Three separate effective dates in three weeks (Sept. 15, 9/29, ongoing) is a content goldmine — timely "what changes when" explainers build trust with shippers scrambling to replan cross-border routes right now.
- Freight-tech and insurtech marketers should target the most exposed segments (cross-border LTL, dairy/beverage shippers, powersports importers) with account-based campaigns ahead of the Sept. 15 and Sept. 29 deadlines.

🔗 Source: FreightWaves, Sept. 9, 2026, https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trump-escalates-canada-trade-war-with-import-bans-federal-contract-restrictions
