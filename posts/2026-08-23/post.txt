⚠️ Freight Layoff Wave: 7,000+ Jobs Cut Across 21 Companies

FreightWaves' latest Freight Distress Report tracked over 7,000 jobs eliminated across freight, logistics, manufacturing and distribution networks in just weeks — and the causes range from a cattle shortage to a capital crunch.

📊 The stats/facts:
• 7,058 confirmed jobs cut across 21 companies in 15+ states (FreightWaves Freight Distress Report, Aug 19)
• Tyson Foods leads with 3,000+ jobs cut, closing plants in Joslin, IL and Eagle Mountain, UT amid a historic cattle shortage
• Wholesale distributor Essendant is cutting 1,278 jobs across 6 states while seeking a buyer or additional capital
• FedEx is permanently closing its Victorville, CA facility Sept. 28 as part of its Network 2.0 restructuring (173 jobs cut across Southern California)
• Daimler Truck (~375 jobs, Portland, OR) and Carlex Glass America (325 jobs, Vonore, TN) are both shifting production out of state

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Buyers across manufacturing, 3PL/distribution, and last-mile are simultaneously cutting cost, not growing — lead with efficiency/ROI/consolidation-proofing messaging over growth pitches for the next quarter
• Essendant's cuts came bundled with "seeking a buyer or additional capital" language — a live reminder to flag churn risk on any account showing distress signals in trade press
• FreightWaves turned scattered layoff headlines into a recurring, brandable "Distress Report" data series that gets picked up by Yahoo Finance and The Loadstar — a playbook for freight-tech and insurtech brands building their own earned-media data series
