⛽ Diesel Just Broke $6/Gallon — A Record That's Never Happened Before

National average diesel hit $6.06 a gallon on Friday, the first time in U.S. history it's crossed that line, as the Iran conflict chokes off crude supply.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National average diesel: $6.06/gallon (Sept 11) — the first-ever break above $6, per AAA/DOE data
• Up from $5.85/gallon just one week earlier; smashes the old record of $5.82 set in June 2022
• Up ~63% year-over-year, from $3.71/gallon a year ago
• Over 60% higher than the $3.76/gallon pre-conflict average from late February 2026
• Driver: crude oil above $100/barrel as Strait of Hormuz shipping is disrupted and Saudi output hits 30-year lows

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Fuel now runs 15-30% of total food-delivery cost — carriers, 3PLs and shippers need messaging that leads with cost mitigation, not just capacity
• This is a ready-made urgency hook for freight-tech/insurtech campaigns: fuel-surcharge automation, route optimization and hedging tools are suddenly a "right now" sale, not a Q1 nice-to-have
• The story's simultaneous run across CNBC, PBS, NPR and every freight trade outlet is itself a PR lesson — a single DOE data point, packaged with a clear record-breaking number, travels fast; have your own stat ready before the next data point drops
