⛽ Diesel Just Hit a New 2026 High — And It's Not Slowing Down

National diesel prices climbed to $5.652/gallon this week, driven by escalating U.S.-Iran tensions over the Strait of Hormuz, pushing fuel costs to their highest point of the year and squeezing carrier margins nationwide.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National average diesel: $5.652/gal (EIA), up 20¢ week-over-week — a new 2026 high
• Up $1.944 vs. this time last year (roughly +53%)
• Regional spread is brutal: West Coast averaging $6.407/gal, with California topping $7.04/gal, vs. $5.35/gal in the Lower Atlantic
• Cause: escalating U.S.-Iran conflict, new sanctions on Iranian trade entities, and Strait of Hormuz instability keeping crude near $80/barrel
• AAA says August 2026 is shaping up to be the most expensive August for fuel on record

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Fuel surcharges are back in every shipper conversation — carriers, brokers, and 3PLs selling "fuel efficiency," "route optimization," or "fixed-rate" offerings have a live, urgent pain point to lead campaigns with right now
• Sharp week-over-week volatility (20¢, two weeks running) is a trust moment: brands that publish transparent, real-time fuel-cost data in their content build credibility faster than generic "we've got your back" copy
• The regional gap ($5.35 vs. $7.04/gal) is a targeting cue — messaging, case studies, and ad spend should be region-specific rather than one national blanket campaign
