🤖 FreightTech's Newest Unicorn: HappyRobot Hits $1.2B After $150M Raise

The AI-agent startup that automates freight's phone calls, emails, and follow-ups just closed a Series C that makes it the fastest-growing name in logistics tech.

📊 The stats/facts:
• $150M Series C closed at a $1.2 billion post-money valuation — unicorn status
• ~$200M raised across three rounds in just 20 months, with 5x growth since its Series B closed in late 2025
• Live inside 150+ enterprise customers, including DHL, Uber Freight, Kuehne+Nagel, and LKW WALTER
• Net dollar retention above 150% — well beyond typical enterprise SaaS benchmarks
• Round led by Prysm Capital and co-led by Eurazeo, with a16z, Y Combinator, Koch Disruptive Technologies, and WaVe-X all returning

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Positioning lesson: HappyRobot didn't market "an AI chatbot" — it sold the elimination of the phone calls, emails, and follow-ups that clog freight ops, a clean example of translating tech capability into a business-outcome pitch
• Reference-customer playbook: landing DHL, Uber Freight, and Kuehne+Nagel as named logos before hitting unicorn status is the credibility-building sequence B2B freight-tech marketers should be studying right now
• The metric that sells: 150%+ net dollar retention, not headline ARR, is the proof point driving both investor and buyer confidence — a signal to lead with expansion revenue in freight-tech narratives
