⛽ Diesel Hits All-Time High of $5.85/Gallon as Iran War Chokes Fuel Supply

National diesel prices just shattered a four-year-old record as war-driven disruptions to the Strait of Hormuz and Russian refineries ripple straight into trucking costs and consumer prices.

📊 The stats/facts:
• U.S. average diesel hit $5.85/gallon on Sept. 4, 2026 (AAA) — topping the prior record of $5.82/gallon set in June 2022
• Diesel is up nearly 60% year-over-year, from $3.76/gallon pre-war (late Feb. 2026) to $5.85 today
• Brent crude has surged past $95/barrel (from ~$70 pre-war) as Iran effectively closed the Strait of Hormuz — roughly a fifth of world oil flow — and Russia extended its diesel export ban through September
• Amazon rolled out a temporary 3.5% fuel-and-logistics surcharge on some third-party sellers; UPS, FedEx, and USPS have added their own fuel-related fees
• Fuel makes up 15-30% of total food costs — July grocery prices were already up 2.7% YoY, with seafood up 7% and fresh fruit up 4.9%

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Every new surcharge (Amazon, UPS, FedEx, USPS) is a live messaging hook — carriers, brokers, and freight-tech vendors can push "surcharge transparency" and fuel-hedging tools right now while shippers are actively re-opening contract talks
• This is a rare moment a macro energy story lands directly on trucking P&Ls — content that ties fuel volatility to concrete cost-per-mile guidance will beat generic "state of freight" posts this week
• The story's overnight syndication across dozens of outlets shows how one hard, record-breaking number travels — a model for freight-tech and insurtech PR teams pitching data-driven stories

🔗 Source: Transport Topics (AP), published September 4, 2026 — https://www.ttnews.com/articles/diesel-hits-record-price
