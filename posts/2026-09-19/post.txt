⛽ Diesel Just Hit an All-Time High — And FMCSA Had to Break Emergency Glass

National diesel prices smashed the previous record this week, and the spike is severe enough that federal regulators just issued their first nationwide hours-of-service waiver since COVID.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National average diesel hit $6.285/gallon (EIA data, Sept 14-15, 2026) — an all-time record high
• Prices jumped nearly 69¢ since Aug 31, including a 32¢ one-week spike
• West Coast average diesel reached $7.25/gallon
• Diesel is now running $2.50+/gallon above where it sat a year ago
• FMCSA issued a 3-month HOS waiver (Sept 16 – Dec 16) letting fuel haulers drive up to 16 hrs/day — the first nationwide waiver since the COVID emergency

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Fuel surcharge messaging is a live wire right now — carriers, 3PLs and freight-tech brands with surcharge-transparency or fuel-hedging tools have a genuine, timely news hook to lead with
• Rising diesel + carriers parking trucks (per DAT iQ) signals a capacity squeeze story — content framing "how we're absorbing volatility" lands while the crisis is breaking, not after
• PR lesson: FMCSA's narrow, time-boxed, clearly-scoped waiver is a crisis-comms model — precise rationale and hard dates beat vague reassurance in a fast-moving story
