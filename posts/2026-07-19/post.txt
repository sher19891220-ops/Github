🚂 The Great Truck-to-Rail Shift Just Hit a Decade High

J.B. Hunt's blowout Q2 2026 earnings just confirmed what freight insiders have been whispering: shippers are abandoning trucks for trains at a pace not seen in over ten years.

📊 The stats/facts:
• J.B. Hunt Q2 2026 revenue hit $3.50B (+19% YoY); net earnings $181.0M, EPS of $1.91 — crushing Wall Street's $1.74 estimate
• Intermodal loads rose 10% YoY, outpacing Class I rail growth (8%) and total North American container volumes (5%)
• Intermodal segment revenue jumped 22% to $1.75B; operating income surged 58% to $150.9M
• Domestic intermodal is now running ~30% cheaper than truckload on contract — far beyond the 10-15% discount typically needed to pull freight off the road
• Eastern U.S. intermodal volumes surged 16% YoY (31% on a two-year comp); container fleet utilization topped 90% for the first time in several quarters

💡 Why marketers should care:
• A 30% cost gap is a ready-made campaign hook — carriers, 3PLs and freight-tech brands can build "switch and save" messaging around a stat this stark while the gap holds
• Eastern U.S. shippers are the hottest audience right now for truck-to-rail conversion content — that's where the two-year growth comp hits 31%
• Earnings season is a built-in content calendar: quarterly reports from bellwethers like JBHT are credible, quotable data hooks for LinkedIn and PR — no need to commission original research to have a stat-backed story this week

🔗 Source: FreightWaves, July 18, 2026, https://www.freightwaves.com/news/intermodals-historic-growth
