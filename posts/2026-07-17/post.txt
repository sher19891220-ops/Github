📦 US Ports Set to Smash All-Time Import Record — Then Fall Off a Cliff

Retailers are racing to beat a July 24 tariff deadline, pushing July container imports to an unprecedented 2.47 million TEUs before volumes are forecast to drop sharply through the fall.

📊 The stats/facts:
• July 2026 imports forecast at 2.47M TEU (+3.3% YoY) — a new all-time monthly record, topping the prior high of 2.4M TEU set in May 2022
• June 2026: 2.33M TEU (+18.7% YoY); May 2026: 2.24M TEU (+14.9% YoY)
• The temporary 10% Section 122 global tariff expires July 24, 2026, with new forced-labor tariffs expected as early as August
• Forecast reverses fast: August 2.22M TEU (-4.5% YoY), September 1.99M TEU (-5.7% YoY), continuing into November
• H1 2026 import volume totaled 12.77M TEU, up just 2% vs. H1 2025 — meaning July's surge is a compressed sprint, not sustained growth

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Urgency window: carriers, 3PLs, and freight-tech vendors have roughly one week to court retailers still racing the July 24 deadline — capacity/booking pitches land now or miss the cycle entirely
• Plan messaging around the drop-off: with imports forecast to fall through November, freight-tech and 3PL marketing should pivot from "capacity crunch" urgency to "smooth the post-peak slowdown" value props by August
• PR/content playbook: this entire narrative traces back to one NRF/Hackett Associates data report, picked up by 9+ trade outlets within two weeks — proof a well-timed report beats a press blast for organic trade-press pickup
