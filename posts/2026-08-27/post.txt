🚛 Gatik Just Raised $200 Million to Put More Driverless Trucks on the Road

Self-driving truck startup Gatik closed its largest funding round yet, doubling down on driverless middle-mile freight for retail giants like Walmart, Kroger and PepsiCo.

📊 The stats/facts:
• $200 million raised, led by Qatar Investment Authority and Koch Disruptive Technologies — Gatik's largest round to date
• Brings total capital raised to roughly $500 million since founding
• More than $600 million in contracted revenue already on the books
• 85,000+ fully driverless orders completed, 99% delivered on time
• Targeting 100+ fully driverless trucks on the road by the end of 2026

💡 Why marketers should care:
• "85,000 driverless orders, 99% on-time" is a ready-made trust hook — B2B freight-tech marketers can borrow this structure (hard reliability numbers over vague "innovation" claims) to win over skeptical enterprise buyers
• Marquee customer names (Walmart, Kroger, PepsiCo, Tyson) did as much PR lifting as the funding figure itself — logo-dropping remains one of the most effective trust signals in freight tech marketing
• With sovereign wealth and industrial capital (QIA, Koch) now backing autonomous freight, expect rival messaging to pivot from "pilot programs" toward "proven revenue" and "contracted backlog" — carriers and 3PLs marketing AI/automation should get ahead of that shift now
