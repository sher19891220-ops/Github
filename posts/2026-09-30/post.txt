🚛 Freight rates are up, but diesel is eating carriers alive

FTR says trucking is in a "chaotic" market: strong spot rates on one side, soaring fuel costs and a widening North American trade war on the other.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Truck driver employment hit its lowest level since 2012 (as of March 2026), with preliminary data pointing lower
• U.S. distillate fuel inventories are at 10-year lows, and proposed diesel export limits add pressure
• Van load posts are running 30% above a year ago (DAT), even as fuel costs climb
• Reefer volumes projected +1.7% in 2026, slowing to +1% in 2027, as pharma and data-center electronics compete with food

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Carriers and brokers are squeezed on margin, so lead with cost-control, fuel and efficiency messaging over growth messaging
• Capacity scarcity is a hook for shipper-facing content: "how to secure reliable capacity when drivers are the constraint"
• The "good news, bad news" framing is a PR lesson: acknowledge both sides of a mixed market and you earn more trust than a pure rate-boom pitch

🔗 Source: TheTrucker.com, Sept 29, 2026 (Cliff Abbott)
