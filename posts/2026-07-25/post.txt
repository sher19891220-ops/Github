📉 Supply Chain Shockwave: 1,222 Jobs Cut Across the Freight Economy in Just Two Weeks

Warehousing, final-mile delivery, and manufacturing firms all pulled the trigger on layoffs between July 10–24 — and 10 freight-dependent companies filed for Chapter 11 in the same window.

📊 The stats/facts:
• 1,222+ jobs eliminated industry-wide, July 10–24, 2026
• Amazon: 494 layoffs as it temporarily closes its 1M-sq-ft Port St. Lucie, FL fulfillment center for a $200M renovation (reopening late 2028)
• Temco Logistics: 223 cuts across GA (92), TX (71), and FL (60) as it exits flatbed final-mile delivery nationwide
• Freight Handlers Inc.: 168 layoffs across five Florida Publix distribution centers
• 10 transportation/freight-dependent businesses filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the same two-week span

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Lead with resilience, not growth hype — logistics buyers right now are anxious about network stability, so messaging around cost-to-serve, consolidation-proofing, and operational continuity will land harder than expansion pitches
• The layoffs are concentrated in final-mile, flatbed, and Florida distribution — a near-term audience of displaced logistics ops and HR leads who may be actively evaluating new software, 3PL partners, or automation; a timely ABM/retargeting segment
• Study the format: FreightWaves runs this as a recurring, named-source "Freight Distress Report" — a repeatable, data-driven trend-report model B2B logistics and freight-tech brands can adapt to build authority and earn newsjacking coverage
