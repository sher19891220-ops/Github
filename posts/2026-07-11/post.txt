🚛 EPA Just Tore Up the Truck Emissions Rulebook
The EPA proposed rolling back Biden-era heavy-duty truck emission standards this week, promising fleets billions in savings — and igniting a fight with environmental groups.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Engine warranty mandates cut from 10 years/450,000 miles to 5 years/100,000 miles
• Emissions-compliance mileage window pushed from 435,000 to 650,000 miles (a 3-year delay); EPA is also weighing scrapping the requirement entirely in favor of a driver alert system
• Projected industry savings: up to $6,000 per new truck, ~$12 billion industrywide
• Estimated NOx pollution increase vs. current rules: +4.2% by 2030, +11.6% by 2055
• Heavy trucks are only 5% of vehicles on U.S. roads but, per the Environmental Defense Fund, the largest source of pollutants linked to asthma, bronchitis, heart attacks and strokes

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Cost-savings hook, ready-made: fleet tech, OEM, and financing marketers can pivot campaigns from "absorb the compliance cost" to "redirect your $6K/truck savings into telematics, fuel efficiency, or driver retention"
• Your compliance content just went stale: any "prepare for 2027 emissions standards" webinar, whitepaper, or sales deck needs an update before a competitor gets there first
• There's a live controversy to plug into: trucking industry vs. environmental groups is an active news fight — sustainability-minded brands (insurtech, ESG reporting, carbon tracking) have an opening to speak to shippers and 3PLs who still want emissions credibility despite the rollback
