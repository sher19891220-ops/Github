🛃 White House Deploys AI to Hunt Down $75B in Tariff-Dodging Shipments

A new federal enforcement report accuses 40+ countries of helping funnel Chinese goods around U.S. tariffs — and the government is rolling out an AI system to catch it before containers even clear customs.

📊 The stats/facts:
• $75 billion in illegally transshipped goods identified between February 2025 and February 2026
• An estimated $19-34 billion in lost U.S. tariff revenue tied to the scheme
• 40+ countries flagged for elevated transshipment risk, including Mexico, Canada, the EU, India, Japan, South Korea, Indonesia, Thailand, Brazil, and Malaysia
• The new "detective border" AI system cross-references shipping routes, factory ownership, production capacity, and port X-ray images to catch mismatched cargo
• Findings are built on two government and three private-sector data sources

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Compliance just became a sellable differentiator — trade-compliance, customs brokerage, and supply-chain visibility vendors can lead with "prove your origin before the AI flags it" messaging to importers sourcing from the 10 named countries
• Account-based campaigns targeting importers/3PLs with exposure to these corridors should name the transshipment risk directly, not go generic
• PR lesson: the White House branded this "The Great Transshipment Scam" — a vivid label plus one big dollar figure ($75B) is what turned a dry enforcement memo into Bloomberg/Fortune-level coverage; freight-tech reports should borrow that playbook
