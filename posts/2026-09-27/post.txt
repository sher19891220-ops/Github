📦 Peak Season Sticker Shock: FedEx & UPS Surcharges Kick In Today

FedEx's and UPS's 2026 peak season surcharges start hitting shipments this weekend (UPS from Sept. 27, FedEx from Sept. 28) — and the fee hikes are steeper than last year's.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Ground Residential surcharge jumps ~23% YoY, from $0.65 (2025) to up to $0.80/package at peak (Nov. 23–Dec. 27)
• Additional Handling fees range $8.80–$11.85 per package depending on the date
• Oversize charges climb to $95.75–$117.25 per package
• Ground Unauthorized Package fees hit $535–$595
• Full surcharge window runs through Jan. 17, 2027, with the steepest pricing Nov. 23–Dec. 27

💡 Why marketers should care:
• E-commerce and D2C brands will pass at least some of this cost to consumers — "free shipping" messaging needs a fresh look before Black Friday/Cyber Monday campaigns launch
• This is a ready-made content hook: freight-tech, TMS, and parcel-audit vendors can lead Q4 campaigns with "protect your margins from peak surcharges" messaging aimed at ops and finance buyers
• Brokers/3PLs marketing to shippers should target companies with residential-heavy, high-return SKUs (apparel, home goods) — they're the segment absorbing the biggest hikes
