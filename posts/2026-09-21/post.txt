⛽ DIESEL JUST BROKE $6.50/GALLON — A NEW ALL-TIME HIGH

Ten days after diesel first cracked $6/gallon, the record keeps falling — and the pace of the climb is now the bigger story than the price itself.

📊 The stats/facts:
• U.S. average diesel hit $6.505/gallon on Sept. 21 (AAA) — the first time the fuel has ever topped $6.50, surpassing the prior 2022 peak
• Diesel has gained more than 87 cents in September alone, and $1/gallon in just four weeks (from $5.5477 a month ago)
• Gasoline is rising in sympathy too — up to $4.48/gallon from $4.10 a month earlier
• Root causes: the US-Iran war still curtailing Strait of Hormuz shipments, a Russian export ban on diesel that may extend through October, and Ukrainian drone strikes knocking out Russian refining capacity
• Economists warn the fuel shock could push home-heating oil costs up 30%+ this winter, spreading well beyond freight into groceries and everyday goods

💡 Why marketers should care:
• The story arc has shifted from "record price" to "record keeps breaking every few days" — content and fuel-surcharge messaging that was accurate a week ago is already stale, so cadence matters as much as the message itself
• Carriers, brokers and 3PLs should be proactively pushing surcharge transparency and fuel-hedging/efficiency offers now, before shippers start asking why invoices moved again
• PR lesson: this is a case study in how a single, easy-to-localize number ($6.50, "first time ever") gets picked up everywhere from Bloomberg to regional outlets — anchor your own comms around one unmistakable stat, not a paragraph of context
