⛽ U.S. Diesel Just Hit an All-Time Record High — $5.85/Gallon

Diesel smashed through the 2022 peak this Labor Day weekend, and truckers say it's already reshaping their cost structure.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National average diesel hit $5.85/gallon Friday, Sept 5 — an all-time U.S. record, surpassing the June 2022 high
• Gasoline hit $4.14/gallon for Labor Day — about $1.00 higher than Labor Day 2025 and above the prior Labor Day record of $3.82 (set in 2012)
• Carrier Volk Transfer reports a 50% jump in average diesel spend since the start of 2026, pushing operating costs up 25-30%
• Root causes: the six-month Iran conflict disrupting the Strait of Hormuz, U.S. refineries running near 98% capacity amid Texas heat, plus reduced refinery output in Russia (drone strikes) and China
• "Your cost per mile jumps up considerably from that." — Ben Froehlich, VP, Volk Transfer

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Every freight-tech, fuel-card, fuel-hedging, and EV/alt-fuel brand has an urgent, real cost-savings hook to lead with right now — budget owners are feeling this pain today, not hypothetically
• Carriers, brokers, and 3PLs should get ahead of shipper anxiety with proactive fuel-surcharge explainers and cost-per-mile calculators before customers start shopping quotes elsewhere
• Content/PR lesson: this story is spreading because it pairs a hard record number with one named operator citing an exact percentage — specificity, not "prices are up," is what earns pickup
