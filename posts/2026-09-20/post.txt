🚨 DIESEL HITS A RECORD $6.49/GALLON — FREIGHT COSTS ARE SPIRALING

A drone strike on Saudi Arabia's East-West Pipeline and an ongoing Strait of Hormuz blockade just pushed U.S. diesel to an all-time high, and trucking's most universal cost input is now rippling through the entire economy.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National average diesel hit $6.4866/gallon on Sept 19 (AAA) — a new record
• That's ~73% higher than the $3.75/gallon pre-crisis baseline from Feb 27, 2026
• U.S. refineries are running at 97% capacity with low reserves heading into harvest and winter maintenance season
• California diesel has already crossed $8/gallon, per Norfolk Southern's chief commercial officer
• Small spot-market carriers are "getting crushed" — limited ability to pass costs through via fuel surcharges (DAT Freight & Analytics)

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Fuel-surcharge automation, TMS visibility, and cost-optimization tools have a real urgency hook right now — shippers are actively re-bidding contracts and hunting for margin protection
• Carrier failure risk is rising fast; factoring, insurtech, and cash-flow solutions can lead with "resilience" messaging aimed at owner-operators and small fleets
• Watch how fast this traveled from trade press (Transport Topics, Overdrive) to CNBC and Euronews in under a week — a reminder that one hard number (a new price record) is what turns a niche freight story into mainstream coverage
