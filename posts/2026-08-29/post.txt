🚨 US-Canada Trade War Erupts: 50% Tariffs Slam Cross-Border Freight

Trade talks collapsed Friday night, and now the two countries that move $67.9B in freight a month are locked in an escalating tariff fight with no end date in sight.

📊 The stats/facts:
• US imposed 50% tariffs on Canadian goods effective 12:01 a.m. ET Saturday, after negotiators failed to close a deal Friday
• Canada's retaliatory tariffs (15%, 25% and 50% tiers on ~$27.6B of US imports) take effect September 8
• June 2026 cross-border freight between the US and Canada totaled $67.9 billion (Bureau of Transportation Statistics)
• The two countries trade roughly $800B in goods and $100B in services annually — deeply integrated in autos, machinery and energy
• Trucking stocks sold off on the news: J.B. Hunt -5.1%, FedEx Freight -4.5%, Landstar -4.1%, Old Dominion -2.4%

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Shippers and carriers are actively anxious right now — "trade war readiness" content (capacity planning, route diversification, surcharge transparency) will outperform generic messaging this quarter
• Cross-border compliance, customs automation and real-time visibility are suddenly urgent buying triggers for freight-tech and insurtech marketers — get in front of procurement teams now, before budgets lock
• Notice how this story travels: trade press is leaning on named quotes from trade lawyers and carrier associations, not press releases — a reminder that a sharp expert soundbite earns more pickup than a company statement
