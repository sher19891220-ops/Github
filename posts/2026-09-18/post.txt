🚛 Schneider Just Cut 76% of Its Carrier Network — And It's a Warning Shot for Freight

As diesel tops $6/gallon and a wave of driver-school closures hits the industry, Schneider National slashed its approved carrier list from 60,000 to just 14,000 — and its CEO says trucking doesn't need more capacity, it needs safer capacity.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Schneider National cut its approved carrier list from 60,000 to 14,000 carriers — a ~77% reduction
• Werner Enterprises forecasts a 10-13% YoY increase in one-way rate per total mile for Q3 2026
• Werner's fleet utilization is up 16% year-over-year
• US diesel prices crossed $6/gallon for the first time on record in September 2026
• Drivers behind the squeeze: a year of licensing enforcement and CDL school closures, plus the Supreme Court's May 2026 ruling exposing brokers to negligent-hiring liability

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Shippers are actively hunting for vetted, compliant carrier partners right now — carrier-safety and compliance-tech marketers have a live, urgent pain point to target, not a hypothetical one
• Double-digit rate increases heading into the 2027 bid season are a timely content hook for brokers/3PLs: budget-planning guides, rate-lock campaigns, early-bid urgency messaging
• Schneider's CEO reframing growth as "we don't need more demand, we need quality capacity" is a positioning lesson B2B freight brands can borrow — lead with quality/safety over volume
