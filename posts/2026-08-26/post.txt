🚨 Canada Slaps 50% Tariffs on $27.6B of US Goods — Trucking Braces for Impact

Trade talks between Washington and Ottawa collapsed last week, and Canada just hit back with retaliatory tariffs taking effect September 8 — escalating a fight that could reshape cross-border freight.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Canada's counter-tariffs: 15%, 25%, and 50% on ~$27.6B of U.S. imports, effective Sept. 8, 2026
• Trump has threatened to raise tariffs on Canadian autos, auto parts, steel, and trucks from 25% to 50% starting Jan. 1, 2027
• ~15,000 commercial trucks cross the U.S.-Canada border daily (5.5M/year), carrying $396B in goods annually
• Trucks account for 55%+ of total U.S.-Canada freight value
• Canada's retaliation list hits seafood, dairy, paper, furniture, apparel, steel and aluminum — though medium/heavy trucks are excluded from this round

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Cross-border freight brands (carriers, brokers, 3PLs near Detroit, Port Huron, and Buffalo) need tariff-impact messaging ready now — shippers are actively re-routing and re-pricing
• Freight-tech and insurtech marketers have a narrow window to pitch tariff-exposure modeling and capacity-planning tools before the Jan. 2027 truck-tariff deadline hits
• It's a live PR speed test: this story jumped from trade press to NPR, CNBC, and Time within a day — have your talking points ready before reporters call
