🚨 Diesel Just Hit an All-Time Record — $6.285/Gallon
The DOE/EIA weekly benchmark used for most fuel surcharges broke its own record again this week, with no sign of the surge letting up.

📊 The stats/facts:
• DOE/EIA weekly retail diesel benchmark jumped 31.8¢/gallon to $6.285/gallon — the highest in the index's history
• Beats the prior all-time high of $6.2694/g set just last week, which itself broke the June 2022 post-Ukraine-invasion record of $5.82/g
• CME ultra-low-sulfur diesel futures settled at $5.262/gallon — the highest futures settlement since the contract began trading in 1978
• Surge driven by refinery outages, a Russian diesel export ban, seasonal maintenance season, and rising heating-oil demand competing for barrels
• Carriers running DOE/EIA-indexed fuel-surcharge programs are seeing automatic rate increases hit shippers this week

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Fuel-surcharge transparency is now a top-of-mind buyer concern — freight-tech and TMS marketers can win attention fast with content/tools that demystify surcharge math or offer real-time cost visibility
• Carriers, 3PLs and brokers have a trust-building moment: proactive, plain-language communication about surcharge changes right now differentiates you from competitors who spring fee hikes on customers silently
• PR lesson: a routine weekly government data release turned into a weeks-long headline story because each new record gave outlets a fresh, quotable number — pegging content to recurring data milestones (not just one-off news) can extend your own campaign's shelf life
