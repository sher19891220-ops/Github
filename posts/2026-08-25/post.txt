🚨 ATA's Chris Spear Steps Down After 10 Years — No Successor Named

Trucking's most powerful lobbying voice just lost its leader: the American Trucking Associations' longtime President & CEO exited abruptly on Friday, leaving a search committee scrambling ahead of major 2027 regulatory deadlines.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Chris Spear departed as ATA President & CEO effective immediately on Fri., Aug. 21, 2026, after informing the Executive Committee of his decision
• He cut short a contract extension that ran through 2029 — walking away roughly 3 years early
• Led ATA for more than a decade, steering it through a 3-year freight recession and high-stakes fights against CARB and EPA emissions mandates
• ATA Chairman Greg Hodgen (CEO, Groendyke Transport) confirmed the departure to members and announced a board search committee — no interim CEO has been named
• The news broke across nearly every major trucking trade outlet within hours — FreightWaves, Transport Topics, CCJ, FleetOwner, Land Line, Truck News, TheTrucker and Bulk Transporter all covered it

💡 Why marketers should care:
• A leadership vacuum at trucking's top trade body opens a messaging window — B2B freight-tech, carrier and 3PL marketers can move thought-leadership content now, before a new CEO resets ATA's advocacy narrative
• Watch who fills this seat ahead of 2027 emissions deadlines: compliance, ESG and insurtech marketers should track the successor search, since ATA's regulatory posture shapes buyer urgency around telematics, compliance software and safety tech
• PR/comms lesson: Hodgen's tightly controlled, single-message rollout ("advocacy agenda remains intact") is a case study in managing a sudden C-suite exit without spooking a 34,000-member federation — worth studying for your own crisis-comms playbook
