🚨 Diesel Just Hit an All-Time High — $5.85/Gallon and Still Climbing

The Iran war has broken the U.S. fuel market: diesel smashed its 2022 record on Friday, and freight companies are already passing the cost down the chain.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National average diesel hit a record $5.85/gallon on Friday, Sept. 4 (AAA) — topping the prior all-time high of ~$5.82 set in June 2022 after Russia invaded Ukraine
• Diesel is up nearly 60% year-over-year, from $2.98/gallon before the U.S.-Israel war on Iran began in late February
• The rally hasn't stopped: EIA's weekly on-highway diesel figure jumped another 36.8 cents to $5.967/gallon on Sept. 7 — a new weekly record
• California diesel is already at $7.70/gallon
• Amazon added a 3.5% fuel surcharge; UPS, FedEx and USPS have all tacked on new fees, citing fuel costs — fuel is 15-30% of total food-shipping costs, and grocery prices are following (seafood +7% YoY, fresh fruit +4.9%)

💡 Why marketers should care:
• This is a live cost-pressure story every shipper, carrier and 3PL is feeling right now — freight tech, TMS, and fuel-hedging vendors have a real-time hook for "control what you can control" messaging
• Insurtechs, brokers and carriers can turn rate/cost volatility into timely thought leadership instead of generic content — the data points above are ready-made for a LinkedIn post or newsletter lead
• PR lesson: one well-sourced AP wire story (with a punchy quote from SemiCab's CEO) got picked up by PBS, CNBC, Washington Post, Al Jazeera and dozens of local outlets — a strong stat + one quotable expert is still the cheapest way to earn broad syndication
