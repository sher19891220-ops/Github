⛽ DIESEL JUST HIT $6.05/GALLON — AN ALL-TIME RECORD

National diesel prices smashed the 2022 record this week as a Middle East conflict chokes off refining capacity, and the pain is already flowing straight into freight invoices.

📊 The stats/facts:
• National average diesel hit $6.05/gallon on Sept. 11 — up from $5.85 a week earlier and $3.70 a year ago (+63% YoY)
• Every major fuel-surcharge benchmark set a record this week: DOE/EIA weekly average $5.967/gal and AAA daily average $5.9424/gal both topped the prior 2022 high of $5.82
• Brent crude topped $105/barrel as Gulf refining capacity offline reached roughly 4M barrels/day amid the conflict
• Fuel makes up 15-30% of total food costs — July grocery prices were already up 2.7% YoY, seafood +7%, fresh fruit +4.9%
• The DOE/EIA weekly average is the benchmark most carriers use to reset fuel-surcharge tables, so surcharge notices are landing on shipper invoices within days, not weeks

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Instant messaging hook for anyone selling fuel efficiency, EV/alt-fuel trucks, route optimization, or fuel-hedging tools — "record diesel" is a live, urgent search and conversation topic right now
• Freight, 3PL, and broker marketers should get ahead of client anxiety: proactive "here's how the surcharge hits your rate" content builds trust before shippers have to ask
• PR lesson: this story spread across trade and mainstream press alike because every outlet could localize one unmistakable headline number — pair your own proof points with a single sharp stat to ride the same wave
