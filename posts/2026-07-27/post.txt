🌉 New $4.7B Bridge Opens TODAY — Detroit-Windsor Freight Corridor Gets Its First New Crossing in a Century

The Gordie Howe International Bridge opened to traffic at noon today, adding a second dedicated span to North America's busiest truck trade corridor.

📊 The stats/facts:
- $4.7 billion megaproject: six lanes, 1.5 miles across the Detroit River, built since 2018
- 400 commercial truck crossings/hour capacity; 2.7–2.9 million truck crossings projected annually
- The Windsor-Detroit corridor already carries roughly 25% of all Canada-U.S. truck trade
- Toll for a typical 5-axle semi: $34.50 with the "Breakaway" discount program vs. $75 on the nearby Ambassador Bridge
- Estimated 850,000 trucker-hours saved per year at this border point

💡 Why marketers should care:
- Carriers, 3PLs, and brokers running Michigan-Ontario automotive lanes now have a concrete "faster, cheaper crossing" claim for campaigns and sales content — first-mover messaging matters before every competitor is saying the same thing.
- Freight-tech and cross-border compliance vendors (ELD, customs brokerage, TMS) have a timely, data-backed news hook to build content around instead of speculation.
- The bridge's toll-discount "Breakaway" program is a live case study in incentive-driven adoption worth studying for any carrier loyalty or lane-commitment program.
