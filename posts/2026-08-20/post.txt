🚨 US-Canada Reach Last-Minute Deal to Pause 50% Tariffs on $20B in Cross-Border Goods

Hours before a 12:01 a.m. deadline, Trump and Canadian PM Mark Carney struck a last-minute pause on Section 338 tariffs that would have hit everything from autos to cheese to hockey sticks moving across the world's busiest trucking border.

📊 The stats/facts:
• 50% tariff was set to hit $20 billion worth of Canadian goods starting Wed. Aug 19 at 12:01 a.m.
• Deal pauses the tariffs for 3 days, through August 22, while a final agreement is finalized
• 330,000 people and $2 billion in goods cross the US-Canada border every single day
• 72% of Canada's exports go to the US — trucking carries the bulk of that freight
• Covered goods ranged from autos, alcohol and dairy/cheese to hockey sticks and tongue depressors

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Tariff whiplash is now a recurring news cycle — B2B freight-tech and insurtech marketers can win attention fast by publishing same-day "what this means for shippers" content while the story is hot, not a week later
• Cross-border carriers and brokers need messaging built around agility and real-time visibility (rate/compliance tools) rather than fixed pricing, since deals can flip in hours
• PR lesson from the coverage itself: this story spread because it was concrete and numeric ($20B, 50%, 3 days) — lead with hard numbers, not vague "trade tension" framing, in your own content
