🚨 Spot Freight Rates Just Flipped the Script — And Truckers Have the Upper Hand for the First Time in 4 Years

DAT Freight & Analytics' June 2026 numbers confirm what shippers have felt all month: van spot rates now beat contract rates, a reversal that hasn't happened since February 2022.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Van spot linehaul rates hit $2.37/mile in June, up 21¢ from May and up 45% year-over-year — the biggest YoY jump since June 2021
• Refrigerated linehaul rose to $2.70/mile (+39% YoY); flatbed hit a record $2.94/mile (+40% YoY)
• Total spot rate for van (including fuel surcharge) reached $3.00/mile; flatbed spot hit an all-time high of $3.69/mile
• Van freight volume (TVI: 262) rose 11% month-over-month but was flat year-over-year — proof this is a capacity story, not a demand boom
• DAT analyst Dean Croke: "Van spot beating contract for the first time in four years... shows real capacity pressure," driven largely by regulatory and immigration-enforcement changes shrinking the qualified driver pool

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Capacity-driven (not demand-driven) rate spikes are a rare, factual hook for freight-tech and TMS marketers to sell rate-volatility protection, dynamic pricing tools, and capacity-matching platforms — the "spot beats contract" inversion is the clearest proof point shippers have seen in years
• Carriers and driver-recruitment marketers can flip the narrative: with capacity this tight, driver leverage and pay are rising — a timely message for recruitment campaigns aimed at owner-operators sitting on the sidelines
• 3PL and broker marketers should get ahead of the contract-renewal conversation now: contract rates historically lag spot by ~6 months, so shippers locking in renewals this year need messaging that reframes "rate increase" as "market-wide capacity correction," not vendor-specific pricing
