🚢 Trump Slaps a 20% Toll on Strait of Hormuz Cargo — Declares US the "Guardian"

As the US-Iran ceasefire collapses after a weekend of missile and drone strikes, President Trump says the US will now charge every ship a 20% cut of cargo value to transit the world's most critical oil and shipping chokepoint.

📊 The stats/facts:
• Trump announced the US will act as "Guardian of the Hormuz Strait," charging a 20% toll on cargo value for vessels transiting the strait, effective immediately.
• Vessel traffic through Hormuz has collapsed to single digits per day, down from the 30-40 tankers and bulkers that transited daily before this week's escalation.
• Brent crude has jumped toward $80/barrel this week as the June ceasefire framework fell apart.
• Asia-to-US ocean container rates were already surging into peak season — Asia-US West Coast rates up 120% and East Coast up 85% since mid-May.
• Trump's quote: "We'll become the guardian of the strait... we can't be expected to do that for nothing."

💡 Why marketers should care:
• Rate volatility is now front-page news, not a footnote — freight-tech and insurtech marketers can lead with "de-risk your Q3 ocean freight budget" messaging while shippers are actively hunting for hedging, visibility, and re-routing tools.
• This is a live, high-attention case study in supply chain fragility — chokepoint-risk content (Hormuz, Suez, Panama) has evergreen value; repurpose today's headlines into a gated guide or lead magnet while search interest is hot.
• A 20% cargo toll raises immediate "who pays, how is it billed" questions — brokers and 3PLs selling transparent landed-cost or total-cost-of-ownership tools have a timely, specific hook for outbound campaigns this week.
