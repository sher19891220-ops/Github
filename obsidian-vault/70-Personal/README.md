# Personal

Same vault, walled-off folder. `CLAUDE.md` instructs Claude never to publish or
externally summarize anything in here.

Suggested notes to start:
- `Health.md` — the thing that goes first when you run seven entities
- `Family.md` — commitments, dates, promises made
- `Money-Personal.md` — separate from the business P&Ls
- `Learning.md` — what you're trying to get better at
- `Someday.md` — the exit / next-venture thinking you never write down

The reason this belongs in the same vault and not a separate app: your business
and personal decisions are actually coupled (cash, time, risk appetite), and the
value of Claude having both in one place is exactly that it can say
"you committed to X at home on the same week you took on Y truck payments."
