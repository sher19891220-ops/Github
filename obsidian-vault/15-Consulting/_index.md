# Consulting

A separate business line: advising other carriers, using the operating knowledge
built running the group.

**Hard boundary.** Everything in this folder is client-confidential. It is not
group data, it never appears in Founder Hub content, and client numbers are never
mixed into group consolidation.

## Why this is worth tracking properly
Consulting is the highest-margin thing in the group — no trucks, no drivers, no
fuel, no claims exposure. It also compounds: every client engagement teaches
something that improves the group's own operation, and vice versa.

But it is the easiest to let drift, because there is no truck sitting idle to
remind you it needs attention.
