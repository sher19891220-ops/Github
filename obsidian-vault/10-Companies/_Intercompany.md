# Intercompany ledger

Every transaction between two entities in the group. These are **related-party
transactions** — entities are titled under different people, so the paper trail
matters, both for tax and for keeping partnerships clean.

Claude: never net these away when consolidating. Show them, then eliminate them
explicitly in a labeled step.
